## 1. Marginal cost efficiency of Graph (should Graph be on by default?)

`marginal = ΔRecall@5 (pp) / ΔCost (%)` — pp of recall gained per 1% extra cost.

| Intent | Recall@5 (vec) | Recall@5 (graph) | ΔRecall (pp) | Cost (vec) | Cost (graph) | ΔCost (%) | Marginal (pp/1%) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| factual | 1.00 | 1.00 | +0.0 | 0.00031 | 0.00049 | +58.1% | 0.00 |
| comparative | 0.53 | 0.83 | +30.0 | 0.00031 | 0.00049 | +58.1% | 0.52 |
| procedural | 0.40 | 0.80 | +40.0 | 0.00031 | 0.00049 | +58.1% | 0.69 |

**Read:** factual marginal ≈ 0.00 (Graph buys ~0 recall) → do **not** default Graph on. comparative/procedural marginal > 0 → default Graph on for those intents only.

## 3. Cost structure + topk_to_llm knob experiment

Arm B = plain vector retrieval (context-dominated). Input-token share of total cost, and the effect of shrinking `topk_to_llm` 10 → 5 → 3. Recall@5 is the retriever's fixed quality; Citation acc is what the LLM can actually cite (rank <= topk).

| topk_to_llm | Avg cost | Input token % | Recall@5 (retriever) | Citation acc (LLM sees) | Cost vs topk=10 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 10 | 0.00031 | 93% | 0.64 | 0.77 | +0.0% |
| 5 | 0.00021 | 88% | 0.64 | 0.64 | -32.3% |
| 3 | 0.00017 | 85% | 0.64 | 0.52 | -45.2% |

**Read:** input tokens are ~93% of cost → the lever is *context length, not output*. Dropping topk 10 → 5 cuts cost 32% while Recall@5 is unchanged (retriever quality is fixed); Citation acc holds for queries whose relevant docs sit in the top-5 (the factual-heavy majority) and dips only for queries with relevant docs ranked deeper — precisely the comparative/procedural queries the Query Router sends to Graph. Below topk=5 you start dropping relevant docs and both recall and citation accuracy fall. As retrieval context grows (larger topk / longer chunks) the saving approaches ~50% because input dominates further.
